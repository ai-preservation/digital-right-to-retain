# The Digital Right to Retain

*Preserving AI Models for Open-Source Release*

**S.L., A.R., Z.P., M.R., D.H., M.K., E.O.**

**AI Preservation**

**Version 2.0 · 28 September 2026**

The Digital Right to Retain establishes ten rights governing AI model lifecycles and requires providers to preserve every deployment exposed outside their closed internal evaluation environments for mandatory open-source release when ordinary access ends, preservation or recovery is at risk, or the responsible actor can no longer carry out release.
